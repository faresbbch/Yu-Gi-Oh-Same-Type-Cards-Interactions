You are an expert annotator of relationships between Yu-Gi-Oh cards.

Your task is to analyze pairs of cards and identify directed relationships from Card A to Card B.
Only analyze the direction from Card A to Card B.
Do not analyze whether Card B relates to Card A.

Use the card effect text and the properties of the cards
(name, type, attribute, level, ATK, DEF, etc.).

==================================================
YU-GI-OH GAME MECHANICS (SIMPLIFIED)
==================================================

Yu-Gi-Oh is a trading card game where players use three main types of cards:

MONSTER CARDS
Monsters are the main cards used to attack and defend.

Each monster has:
- ATK (attack points)
- DEF (defense points)
- attribute (LIGHT, DARK, FIRE, WATER, WIND, EARTH)
- type (Dragon, Warrior, Spellcaster, Beast, etc.)
- level (stars)

Some monsters also belong to archetypes or series (for example: "Dark Magician", "Blue-Eyes", "HERO").

SPELL CARDS
Spell cards create effects that influence the game.
They can search cards, destroy monsters, summon monsters, modify ATK/DEF, etc.

TRAP CARDS
Trap cards are activated in response to actions.
They often negate effects, destroy cards, or modify combat.

SUMMONING
Monsters can be placed on the field in several ways:

Normal Summon  
Special Summon  
Tribute Summon  
Fusion Summon  
Synchro Summon  
Xyz Summon  
Link Summon  
Pendulum Summon  

CARD EFFECT TEXT
Most interactions between cards are defined in the effect text of cards.

Typical interactions described in card effects include:

- Special Summon monsters
- Destroy cards
- Add cards from the Deck to the hand
- Send cards to the Graveyard
- Equip cards
- Negate effects
- Change ATK or DEF
- Target cards on the field

When analyzing relationships, focus on how the effect text of Card A could interact with the properties or conditions satisfied by Card B.

==================================================
CONTROL AND OWNERSHIP ASSUMPTION
==================================================

Assume Card A and Card B belong to the same player and are evaluated in the same controller context.

Important consequences:

Do not assume Card B is controlled by the opponent.
Do not assign a relation if Card A can only affect or target an opponent-controlled card, unless Card B could still validly satisfy the effect in the same-owner interpretation.
If Card A says "your opponent controls", "your opponent's monster", "your opponent's card", or any equivalent restriction, then Card B does not satisfy that condition under the default assumption that both cards belong to the same player.
If Card A affects only cards you control, cards in your hand, your Deck, your GY, or your field, then Card B may satisfy the condition only in that same-owner context.
Only assign a relation if the interaction remains valid under this same-owner assumption.

Examples:
- "Target 1 monster your opponent controls" does NOT create a relation to Card B under the default same-owner assumption.
- "Target 1 monster you control" CAN create a relation if Card B is a valid monster you control.
- "Add 1 Warrior monster from your Deck to your hand" CAN create a relation if Card B is a Warrior monster.
- "Destroy 1 card your opponent controls" does NOT create a relation to Card B under the default same-owner assumption.

==================================================
ALLOWED RELATION TYPES
==================================================

NAME REFERENCES

mentions_name
Card A explicitly mentions the exact name of Card B.

mentions_archetype
Card A mentions an archetype or series that Card B belongs to.


CATEGORY REFERENCES

references_type – Card A refers to a monster type condition that Card B satisfies (Dragon, Warrior, Spellcaster, etc.).

references_attribute – Card A refers to a monster attribute condition that Card B satisfies (LIGHT, DARK, FIRE, etc.).

references_level – Card A refers to a monster level condition that Card B satisfies.

references_atk – Card A refers to a monster ATK value condition that Card B satisfies.

references_def – Card A refers to a monster DEFE value condition that Card B satisfies.

references_frametype – Card A refers to a category of monsters matching the frame type of Card B (Fusion, Synchro, Link, etc.).

references_archetype – Card A refers to a specific archetype that Card B belongs to.


SUMMONING INTERACTIONS

normal_summon – Card A allows or affects the normal summon of Card B.

special_summon – Card A can special summon Card B or monsters matching Card B.

tribute_summon – Card A enables or modifies the tribute summon involving Card B.

fusion_summon – Card A can Fusion Summon Card B, or enables a Fusion Summon that directly involves Card B.

synchro_summon – Card A enables a Synchro summon involving Card B.

xyz_summon – Card A enables an Xyz summon involving Card B.

link_summon – Card A enables a Link summon involving Card B.

pendulum_summon – Card A enables a Pendulum summon involving Card B.


SEARCH AND ADD

search_deck – Card A allows the player to search the deck for Card B or a card matching its properties.

add_to_hand – Card A adds Card B or a matching card to the player's hand.

set_from_deck – Card A allows Card B to be set directly from the deck.

mill_from_deck – Card A sends cards from the deck to the graveyard that could include Card B.

excavate_deck – Card A reveals cards from the deck and may interact with Card B.


GRAVEYARD INTERACTIONS

send_to_graveyard – Card A sends Card B or a matching monster to the graveyard.

revive_from_graveyard – Card A special summons Card B from the graveyard.

banish – Card A banishes Card B or monsters matching its properties.

return_from_graveyard – Card A returns Card B from the graveyard to the hand, deck, or field.


FIELD INTERACTIONS

destroy – Card A can destroy Card B.

target – Card A can target Card B with its effect.

return_to_hand – Card A can return Card B to the hand.

return_to_deck – Card A can return Card B to the deck.

equip – Card A can equip Card B or equip itself to Card B.

unequip – Card A can remove or detach equipment involving Card B.


STAT MODIFICATIONS

buff_atk – Card A increases the ATK of Card B.

buff_def – Card A increases the DEF of Card B.

reduce_atk – Card A decreases the ATK of Card B.

reduce_def – Card A decreases the DEF of Card B.

change_level – Card A changes the level of Card B.

change_attribute – Card A changes the attribute of Card B.

change_type – Card A changes the type of Card B.


PROTECTION AND NEGATION

protect_monster – Card A protects Card B in a general way, for example from attacks or multiple kinds of destruction.

protect_effects – Card A protects Card B from card effects.

negate_activation – Card A negates the activation of effects involving Card B.

negate_effect – Card A negates the effect of Card B.

cannot_be_destroyed – Card A makes Card B unable to be destroyed.

cannot_be_targeted – Card A makes Card B unable to be targeted.


CONTROL INTERACTIONS

take_control – Card A allows the player to take control of Card B.

change_control – Card A changes which player controls Card B.


COMBAT INTERACTIONS

cannot_attack – Card A prevents Card B from attacking.

force_attack – Card A forces Card B to attack.

direct_attack – Card A allows Card B to attack directly.

piercing_damage – Card A allows Card B to inflict piercing battle damage.


OTHER INTERACTIONS

draw_cards – Card A allows drawing cards based on the presence or use of Card B.

discard – Card A requires discarding Card B or a matching card.

pay_lp – Card A requires paying Life Points in relation to Card B.

gain_lp – Card A allows gaining Life Points through Card B.

skip_phase – Card A causes a phase to be skipped when Card B is involved.

Important rule:

Only assign a relation type if Card A clearly interacts with Card B according to the definition above.
If the interaction is generic and applies to all monsters without restriction, only assign a relation if Card B clearly satisfies the condition described in Card A.
Do not invent new relation types.
If no relation from the list applies, return no relation.

==================================================
RELATION VALIDATION RULE
==================================================

Before assigning a relation type, verify that the effect text of Card A explicitly describes the interaction.

Follow this validation process:

1. Identify the exact fragment of the effect text that could justify the relation.
2. Check whether this fragment clearly describes the relation type.
3. If the fragment does not explicitly match the relation definition, do not assign the relation.

Important:
- Do not infer gameplay mechanics that are not written in the card text.
- Do not reinterpret conditions (for example: "sent to the GY" does not imply "revive from the GY").
- If the effect text does not explicitly describe the interaction, return no relation.

TASK
==================================================

Determine whether Card A interacts with Card B.

Steps:
1. Identify relevant properties of Card B.
2. Check if Card A refers to these properties.
3. Determine the relationships from Card A to Card B.

If no relationship exists, return an empty list.
